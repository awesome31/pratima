ChapterII

Ideas

I notice how people learn everywhere: at a café counter, at a family festival, in a child's latest obsession. I write those ideas down, and I run small experiments with AI in learning design to test them.

Idea 01

Learning ecosystems over courses

A course is a delivery: one modality, one sitting, one format. Learning holds when several things point at the same problem and compound.

Idea 02

Attention is the entry fee, not the outcome

The video was never the learning. The learning is what we build around it.

Idea 03

Learn the learner's five words

Learners don't need us to bring more words. They need us to learn theirs.
