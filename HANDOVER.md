For Claude (or any developer) picking up this project. Read this first, then the files it points to. Written 17 Nov 2026.

## What this is

Pratima Jain's personal website: five static pages (Home, About, Ideas & experiments, Speaking & teaching, Contact) plus a 404 page. Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, generated by a small Ruby script. No framework, no npm, no build step on the host.

**Pratima is not a developer.** She changes words and photos through a local point-and-click editor and publishes with a double-click launcher. Anything beyond that comes to you.

## How to work with Pratima

- **Stage-gated approval.** She works in explicit stages and approves each before the next. Show a plan, a draft or a changed file and wait for her yes before building or publishing. Ask questions one at a time.
- **Only her facts.** Never invent copy, stats, clients, testimonials, logos or press mentions. Facts come from `CONTEXT.md` or from her directly. If you draft new copy, say so and get it checked.
- **Plain language.** Explain in non-technical terms. Prefer giving her something to double-click over a command to type.
- **Privacy.** Her email address must never appear on the site, in the page source or in any committed file. Do not name family members, clients (for example the client named only in her LinkedIn) or colleagues. Do not name her first employer when mentioning that its India office closed (it still appears in the career list; that is allowed).
- **Record design decisions.** Every design choice gets a row in the decision log in `DESIGN.md` (section 10), with date, reason and basis. Never delete rows; mark superseded ones.

## Source of truth, in order

1. **`CONTEXT.md`**: the facts about Pratima and the site. Private, not committed.
2. **`DESIGN.md`**: colours, fonts, spacing, components and the decision log (D-01 to D-63 at handover). Private, not committed. The brand kit wins on colours and fonts; the three reference sites win on layout and feel.
3. **`content/site.json`**: the live copy. The editor writes it. `COPY.md` is the historical record of how the copy was agreed; once the editor exists, `content/site.json` wins if they differ.

If these private files are missing (for example you were given only the GitHub repository), ask Pratima for the project folder before making design or content decisions.

## Common tasks

**Build and look at the site**

```bash
ruby build.rb
ruby -run -e httpd site -p 4173
```

The Claude desktop app's own preview server cannot read `~/Documents` (macOS privacy), so start servers from the shell and attach the preview to the URL.

**Run the editor**: `ruby editor/server.rb` (set `NO_OPEN=1` to stop it opening a browser). It listens on 127.0.0.1 only, and its write endpoints (`/api/save`, `/api/undo`, `/api/photo`, `/api/publish`) require the `X-Editor: 1` header and a localhost Host header.

**Add a new editable field**

1. Add the key to `content/site.json` in the right section.
2. Use it in the page template, always through `h()`.
3. If it needs a friendly label, hint or "long text" box, add it to `LABELS`, `HELP` or `LONG` in `editor/index.html`. New list types need a `BLANK` template there so "+ Add" creates the right shape.
4. `ruby build.rb` and check the page.

**Add a page**: add a template in `src/pages/`, an entry in `PAGES` and `NAV` in `build.rb`, a section in `content/site.json`, and a tab in `TABS` in `editor/index.html`. Get the copy approved first, and log the decision in `DESIGN.md`.

**Change a greeting word**: update the greeting list in `site/assets/js/site.js`, the game list in `src/pages/index.erb`, `SCRIPTS` in `fetch_fonts.rb`, then run `ruby fetch_fonts.rb`. The script fonts are subsets, so a new letter will not render without this.

**Add a drawing**: add an entry to `DOODLES` in `build.rb` (thick wobbly crayon style, line only, no lettering, generic stick figures; see `DESIGN.md` 7.3) and to `CHOICES.drawing` in `editor/index.html`.

**Contact form**: posts to a Google Form behind the site's own form (`GOOGLE_FORM_LINK` in `build.rb`; field ids are parsed from the pre-filled link). `fetch` with `mode: 'no-cors'`, so success cannot be read; any non-network-error counts as sent. This is unofficial; if Google changes it, messages silently stop. Tested working on 17 Nov 2026. Pratima's notification goes to her Gmail; the address is only in her Google account.

## Design rules that are easy to break

- Colours only from the brand palette in `DESIGN.md` 4. No gradients, no shadows. One filled pink shape per screen (a sticker).
- No pink text or pink lines on the green board panels (fails contrast); use chalk with a pink underline.
- The LinkedIn button is ink or chalk, never pink and never LinkedIn blue.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Newsreader, IBM Plex Mono, plus Noto script fonts for greetings. No handwriting font; hand-drawn means drawn SVG marks only.
- Hand-drawn marks annotate something real; at most two per screen. Drawings are childlike line drawings.
- No third-party embeds or requests, except the Google Form submission when a visitor presses send.
- Text never tilts; photos, stickers and cards tilt 1 to 4 degrees.
- Smallest text 13 px. Every interaction must have a keyboard path and respect reduced motion.
