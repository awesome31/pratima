Publish

Publishing sends your saved changes to GitHub, and Vercel puts them online a minute or two later.

Send a preview

Puts the changes on a private preview link. Your real website does not change.

Go live

Replaces what visitors see on your real website. Do this once you are happy with the preview.

How this works

  1. Pick a page on the left.
  2. Change the words in the boxes. Click a title with a + to open it.
  3. Press Save and preview. The preview on the right shows the result.
  4. Not what you wanted? Press Undo last save. Each press steps back one save.
  5. Happy? Press Publish, send a preview first, then go live.

Lists (ideas, career rows, talks): use ↑ and ↓ to reorder, × to remove, and the dashed button to add one.

Leave a box empty to hide that line on the site. An empty link hides its button.

Nothing here changes the design. Colours, fonts, layout, the greetings and the drawings are fixed. To change those, open this folder in Claude and ask; the file HANDOVER.md tells Claude everything it needs.

More detail is in README.md in the project folder.
