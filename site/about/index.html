About

From pharmacist to learning designer.

I'm Pratima Jain, a learning experience designer and project lead at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) in Mumbai. I trained as a pharmacist, and for more than nine years I have helped life sciences teams learn.

How I got here

I discovered my love for learning and for training people at the Youth Trainer's Academy in 2013, and again while coordinating training for the International Pharmaceutical Students' Federation. I was studying pharmacy and management at the time, but I already knew I wanted to work in learning and development.

The chance came earlier than I expected. I began my career in scientific marketing. When my first employer closed its India office in 2017, I took it as my opening and joined TCS as a trainer. I have been in learning ever since.

A learning engineer in the making

A learning engineer builds AI agents, software and automations so that people don't have to learn the task in the first place. Take a firm that needs flyers in its own brand style. I would rather build it a marketing coach that knows the brand guidelines than teach everyone graphic design.

It is the shortest possible route to the business objective, with the human touch and the learning process still at the heart of it. I'm getting there one experiment at a time, which is why I call myself a learning engineer in the making.
